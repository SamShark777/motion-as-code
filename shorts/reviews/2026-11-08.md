## 3 hook/topic rules for next week (for new scripts; hypotheses, not proven)

1. **Line 1 = a concrete moment with a number or a reversal.** Write "paid $50, now wants $200", "twenty minutes LATE, so he's unreliable", "walks in and FORGETS why". Not a mood ("Three A M worries…") or a quiz question ("Do you always hunt for the best option?"). Most of this week's top performers opened this way (milo-snap-judgment, dev-armchair, june-doorway, ikea-effect); the weakest didn't.
2. **Default to the Experiment-story shape:** a character runs into a real study's situation, the "But…" names the study, and the payoff is a tip someone can screenshot. Experiment story is the most consistent pattern (n = 6: IG ret 41%, TT ret 26%, YT ~1,000). The one Short with real shares (omar-knew-it, 10%) ended on a practical tip.
3. **Hold the length: ≤ 75 words / ≤ 40 s for ElevenLabs voices, ≤ 85 words for Heart.** This week's ElevenLabs Shorts ran 80–99 words and 45–55 s. Over 45 s, Instagram retention halves (24% vs 42% at ≤ 35 s, r = −0.58, n = 38). The Overthinking and "Psychology says" patterns can't be judged fairly until they come in shorter.

These shape new scripts only. Per the pattern-test rules, the queue order, the existing scripts and the runbook rules were not changed.
