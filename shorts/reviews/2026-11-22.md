## 3 hook/topic rules for next week (for new scripts; hypotheses, not proven)

1. **Make the viewer commit in line 2:** "Pause and pick: A or B?" or "Would you…?", and make the reveal about *their* answer. milo-everyone-agrees (IG 1,333 / TT 776, both at 37–38% retention) did this. Aim for about 1 in 4 scripts as a `test`, so the n grows past 2.
2. **Line 1 = a character's concrete action or quoted line that the viewer can see going wrong.** Examples: a text ("Nice job, genius"), a bet, a must-be-perfect night, a paid ticket. Not a trait or a state ("reads friends like a book", "nods along", "pictured the dream"). The week's top TikToks (804, 790) and YouTube (1,010) all opened this way. The stalled ones opened on a state.
3. **Keep signs-list going, but at ≤ 42 s, and swap pain for shock or proof in about 1 in 3 scripts.** signs-list has now produced a strong result on each platform, but at 43–48 s its Instagram retention is only 22–24%. Across all Shorts, under 38 s keeps 30%. 14 of 17 scripts were pain-lean again, so the lean comparison is still blocked.

These shape new scripts only. Per the pattern-test rules, the queue order, existing scripts and runbook rules were not changed.
