## 3 hook/topic rules for next week (for the script writer; hypotheses to test, not proven)

1. **Alternate question and claim hooks.** At least 1 in 3 new scripts opens with a flat, surprising claim instead of a question (e.g. "Your memory edits itself every time you recall it."). Without that, the question-vs-claim comparison can't happen.
2. **Put a concrete number, person or scene in the first line.** "93% of drivers…", "a man in a gorilla suit…", not "Can familiarity influence liking?". Abstract-concept openers ("When do we…", "Can X influence Y?") are out.
3. **Vary the first word of titles.** No more than 1 of each day's 3 titles starts with "Why"; use number-led and "You…" titles too, so title wording can be compared once stats arrive.

These only shape new scripts (topics, hooks and titles), so they stay inside the format freeze.
