1. **Hard cap 32 s: ≤ 70 words for ElevenLabs voices, ≤ 85 for Heart.** ElevenLabs runs ~35% longer. ≥ 40 s Shorts held 25% on Instagram against 42% for ≤ 32 s, and 12 of 20 went over this week.
